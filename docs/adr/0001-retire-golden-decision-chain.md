# Retire the golden decision chain

Glife no longer uses the golden-001 step1–step4 Review Decision → Reviewed Draft path; the active workflow is OMR inference → human-corrected tokens → Training Candidate. The unused tracked chain was removed, while pass1, pass2, review metadata, and required local assets remain as the OMR A/B baseline; future architecture work must not deepen or restore the retired chain unless the product returns to event-level review.
